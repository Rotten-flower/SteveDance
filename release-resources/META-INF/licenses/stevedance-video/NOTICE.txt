SteveDance media decoder uses FFmpeg 7.1.2, LGPL-2.1-or-later.
Official corresponding source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.2.tar.xz
FFmpeg is statically linked into the replaceable stevedance-media.dll JNI library.
The JNI source and build script are in tools/native; corresponding source and relink archives accompany the local candidate in dist/native-source.
No external codec download is performed during playback. Original game assets retain their owners rights.
